How we label stories

Every AINN story carries a mark showing how much of it we can stand behind. The mark is set by an editor, not the writer, and it changes as reporting changes.

Verified

AINN saw the document, ran the test, or confirmed it with two independent sources.

Company claim

A lab or vendor says so. We have not been able to test it ourselves yet.

Reported

Sourced to named people or a credible outlet, but not independently confirmed by us.

Unconfirmed

We are publishing because it matters and it is circulating. Treat it as unsettled.
